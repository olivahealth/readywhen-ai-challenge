# Elena Conti, Lume Locations

Elena owns Lume Locations, a location and production agency in Milan. Brands and ad agencies hire Lume to find the place for a shoot and to run the production around it: the venue, the permits, the crew, the catering, the vans, the insurance. Every job is a shoot with a client, a venue, a date and a budget.

She runs the business from her inbox (elena@lumelocations.it). Her team handles most of the day-to-day and copies her in; clients, venues and her advisers write to her directly. It is Friday 18 September 2026, 09:00 in Milan.
